Frequently Asked Questions - Customer Support

How do I reset my password?
To reset your password, click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your registered email address and you will receive a password reset link within 5 minutes. The link expires after 24 hours. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

What is your refund policy?
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. If you are not satisfied with our service, you can request a full refund within 30 days of your purchase. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval. To request a refund, go to Settings > Billing > Request Refund, or contact our support team.

How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
To change your plan, navigate to Settings > Subscription > Change Plan. Select your desired plan and confirm the change. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual enterprise plans. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.

How do I contact customer support?
You can reach our support team through:
- Live chat: Available 24/7 from the bottom-right corner of any page
- Email: support@company.com (response within 4 hours)
- Phone: 1-800-555-0199 (Mon-Fri, 9am-6pm EST)
- Support tickets: Submit through your dashboard

What are your business hours?
Our AI support is available 24/7. Human support agents are available Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm EST. Enterprise customers have access to priority 24/7 human support.

How do I cancel my subscription?
To cancel your subscription, go to Settings > Subscription > Cancel Plan. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period. No partial refunds are provided for mid-cycle cancellations.

I am experiencing login issues. What should I do?
Try these steps:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
2. Disable browser extensions temporarily
3. Try logging in using an incognito/private window
4. Ensure your browser is up to date
5. If the issue persists, contact support with a screenshot of the error
